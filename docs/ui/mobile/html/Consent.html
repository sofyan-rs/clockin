Before you start

ClockIn collects a few things to confirm your attendance. Here's what and why.

  • A selfie when you clock in and outConfirms it was you. Stored privately and deleted after 12 months.
  • Your location, only at clock-in and clock-outChecks you are at the office. Never tracked in the background.
  • This phone’s IDLinks your account to one phone so no one can clock in for you.

You can see your own attendance data anytime in History. Read the privacy policy
